What We Offer

📚

Wide Book Collection

Browse across 12 categories — from Fiction and Science to Self-Help and Thriller. New titles added regularly.

🔄

Easy Borrowing

Search for any book online, borrow it in one click, and return it at the library desk. No queues, no paperwork.

📅

Due Date Tracking

Your dashboard shows countdown timers for every borrowed book so you never miss a due date.

💰

Transparent Fines

Fines are calculated automatically at ₦100 per overdue day. Pay at the library and the admin clears your record instantly.

🔐

Secure Accounts

Your account and borrow history are protected by JWT-based authentication. Only you can see your records.

🌙

Dark Mode

Toggle between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved so it's consistent every time you log in.

How It Works

1

Create an Account

Sign up with your name, email, and password. Your member account is ready immediately.

2

Browse the Catalogue

Search or filter by title, author, or category. Each book shows how many copies are currently available.

3

Borrow Online

Click "Borrow" on any available book. A 14-day period starts immediately and you can track it from your dashboard.

4

Return at the Library

Bring the physical book to the library desk before the due date. If you return it on time, no fine applies.
